# Design System: 8x Meets

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Lilac Studio"**

8x Meets is a recording studio for conversations, built in the 8x family's lilac world. Every person is a voice and every voice is a track: a colour, a lane, a ring. The system exists to make that visible while people talk (the silk ring on whoever is speaking, a halo that swells with their voice, their colour on every surface that is theirs) and afterwards (the multitrack "who spoke when", voice posters on every recording, a transcript tinted per speaker). It refuses the grey meeting-app grid: no wall of equal black rectangles, no phone cameras cropped to fit, no grey chrome.

The world comes in two lights that share one silk. Lilac day is a mist ground lit from the top by two soft lights, lavender and pale blue; lilac night is deep violet ink with the same silk footage, re-graded rather than filtered. Reading happens on paper (white by day, night panels after dark); anything that floats over moving imagery is glass. Every control is a pill or a circle, and containers step their radii down as they nest, from 34px heroes to 9px tooltips.

Density follows the task. The dashboard and archive are roomy (a 1240px column, 46px between sections); the room is full-bleed and tight, and the stage takes everything the header, dock and side panel leave it. Colour is quiet by default: violet does the acting, neutrals do the holding, and red and amber appear only when something is recording or live, someone is leaving or deleting, your own mic is off, the connection is failing, or someone is asking to speak.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two first-class lights driven by one set of custom properties; `data-theme` on `<html>` flips everything, set before first paint.
- Full-bleed silk video (a day file and a separately graded night file) as the brand's light source; no page sits on a flat ground.
- Glass pills over moving imagery, paper panels for reading.
- Geist at weight 300 for display, Geist Mono for anything measured.
- A track colour per voice, most distinct first, never red, amber or green.
- One easing, an exponential ease-out, across three durations.

## Colors

A lilac-and-ink neutral range carrying one violet for action, two signal colours (red, amber) for state, and eight track colours reserved for voices.

### Primary
- **8x Violet** (`violet` #6147c7 day, #7259ec night): every action. Primary buttons, the send button, a switch that is on, the selected category, screen share when on, a pinned tile, chapter dots. Hover deepens in both lights (#5236bb by day, #6a50e6 by night) so white text on it stays above 4.5:1. Without an action it marks calm states: your own chat bubbles, unread badges, the lobby's waiting pill and the room's lobby count, a Processing chip, the on-air bars, a healthy connection.
- **Violet Type** (`violet-text` #5a3fc4 day, #b3a5ff night): violet as text: the last word of the door headline ("remember."), in-page links ("Open the archive"), timestamps in chapters, the Ask spark icon, transcript time links, the admin role.
- **Violet Wash and Violet Edge** (`violet-soft`, `violet-line`): selected and focused states. Focus rings are a 4px `violet-soft` spread with a `violet-line` border; selected type cards, active dock panels, answer boxes and your leaderboard row sit on the wash.
- **Silk** (`silk-violet` #8b74ff, `silk-blue` #6fa3ff, `silk-orchid` #f0a4e6): the `--silk` gradient, `linear-gradient(115deg, #8b74ff 0%, #6fa3ff 42%, #f0a4e6 78%, #8b74ff 100%)`. It appears in exactly three places: the speaking ring on a room tile, the Ask bar's border on focus, and the processing progress fill.

### Secondary
- **Event Blue** (`blue` #4d68d4 day, #8aa2ff night): events (webinars) and moderators: the Event chip and the moderator role pill, always as text on a 12% wash of itself.

### Tertiary
- **Tape Red** (`rec` #e5484d day, #f0555a night; `rec-text` #c9353a / #ff8a8e; `rec-soft` wash): recording and live (chips with a pulsing dot), leaving (the Leave control, End meeting), your own muted mic in the dock, a poor or lost connection, destructive actions (delete on hover, danger menu items) and sign-in errors.
- **Hand Amber** (`hand` #f2a51f with `hand-ink` #3b2600, the same in both lights): a raised hand. The hand chip on a tile, the amber ring around a hand-raiser's tile, the dock's hand button while raised, the Hands badge, and the banner when the presenter drops out.
- **Caution Amber** (`warn` #9a6100 day, #f4b544 night, with `warn-soft`): the fair-connection state of the connection pill, and nothing else. (Amber's other job is the raised hand, `hand` #f2a51f.)

### Neutral
- **Lilac Mist** (`bg` #f2f0fb) / **Violet Ink Night** (`bg-night` #0c0a1a): the page ground, under the mist lights or the silk on every page; only the room's header strip shows it flat. Also the browser `theme-color`.
- **Lavender Light and Pale-Blue Light** (`glow-a`, `glow-b`): the two radial lights at the top of every `mist` page.
- **Paper and Night Panel** (`surface` #ffffff / #16142a), then `surface-2` (raised fields, type cards), `surface-3` (wells: chips, soft buttons, tracks, bubbles), `surface-4` (pressed wells, switch off, counts).
- **Glass** (`glass`, `glass-strong`, `glass-line`): translucent paper by day, translucent night panel after dark, always with a light edge.
- **Studio Ink** (`ink` #222439 / #f1efff) and its steps `ink-2` (secondary text), `ink-3` (muted, icons at rest), `ink-4` (placeholders and incidental meta only).
- **Hairlines** (`line`, `line-2`, `line-3`): borders at 8%, 13% and 22% of ink by day, white at 7%, 11% and 20% by night. `line-3` is also the scrollbar thumb and the sheet handle.
- **House Lights Down** (`screen` #0b0a18 / #05040d): the stage ground while someone presents.
- **Stage and Tile** (`stage-a`, `stage-b`, `tile`, `tile-line`): the room's own ground, declared in `templates/index.html`.
- **Inverted Ink** (`toast-bg`, `toast-ink`): alias of ink on paper, flipped. Toasts, tooltips and the Continue with Google button use it, so they read dark by day and light by night.
- **Scrim** (`scrim`): violet at 20% by day, near-black at 60% by night, under sheets and the phone side panel.

### Voice tracks
Eight colours, one per voice, with brighter night twins: **Violet** `t0`, **Blue** `t1`, **Teal** `t2`, **Rose** `t3`, **Fuchsia** `t4`, **Sky** `t5`, **Indigo** `t6`, **Plum** `t7`. They are assigned most distinct first in the order **0, 2, 3, 1, 4, 5, 6, 7** (violet, teal, rose, blue, fuchsia, sky, indigo, plum). In the room the order follows joining, and you are seated first, so your own voice is violet. In the archive it follows talk time, so the longest talker is violet. Code sets `--tc: var(--tN)` on the element and components mix from it (`color-mix(in oklab, var(--tc) 30%, transparent)`); `Meets.track(rank)` in `static/meets.js` and `TRACK_ORDER` in the room are the only mappings. Generated initial avatars paint the day hex in both lights.

Contrast as built: most pairs clear 4.5:1 (`ink-4` on the day mist 5.11 and on white 5.76; `ink-3` on a well 6.13 day and 6.89 night; white on violet 6.49 by day; every track colour against white or the night panel 4.77 or better). White on night violet is 4.82 and day `warn` text is 5.14 on white and 4.5 on its own tint, so every text pair in both lights clears 4.5:1.

### Named Rules
**The Violet Acts Rule.** Every action is violet, and no action takes another colour except red for leaving and deleting. Where violet appears without an action, it is calm (selection, focus, progress, something of yours, the brand's own emphasis), never a warning.

**The Loud Means Live Rule.** Red and amber carry state and nothing else: red for recording, live, leaving or deleting, your own muted mic and a failing connection; amber for a raised hand and for caution. Other hue comes only from violet, event blue and the voice tracks.

**The Track Rule.** Voices take `t0` to `t7` in the order 0, 2, 3, 1, 4, 5, 6, 7 and never red, amber or green, so a voice can never be mistaken for a state.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geist, variable 300 to 700 from Google Fonts (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Geist (same stack)
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono, 400 to 600 (with ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace)

**Character:** One family does all the talking, light and tightly tracked at display size, quietly medium at UI size; its mono twin appears only where something is counted or timed. The pairing reads engineered but warm, which keeps a violet world from turning sugary.

### Named Rules
**The Light Display Rule.** Big type is light: display at 300 with -0.04em tracking, headings at 400. Weight 600 is kept for counts, badges and speaker names; nothing is bolded for emphasis.

**The Mono Means Measured Rule.** Geist Mono only for codes, clocks, timestamps, durations and percentages, always with tabular numerals. Prose, labels and placeholders stay in Geist.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Every label, button and heading is sentence case. The build has no uppercase or letter-spaced labels; role pills only capitalize their first letter.

## Layout

**Pages.** The dashboard and archive share one frame: a sticky 68px topbar and a centred column (`max-width: 1240px`, padding 8px 28px 110px; 4px 14px 90px on phones). Heroes and bands are full-width silk panels inside that column. Sections sit 46px apart (36px on phones) with 16px card gaps. The door is its own frame: a 1400px maximum, a 468px card column beside a flexible studio column (gap clamp(32px, 6vw, 110px), gutters clamp(20px, 5vw, 64px)), stacking at 980px.

**Grids.** Meeting cards: `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(290px, 1fr))`, one column on phones. Recording posters: `minmax(250px, 1fr)` on the dashboard (two columns on phones), `minmax(270px, 1fr)` with a 24px by 18px gap in the archive. A recording page is `minmax(0, 1fr)` plus a clamp(340px, 30vw, 420px) transcript column (gap 20px), and the transcript is sticky (top 88px, height to the viewport minus 108px) until the columns stack at 1080px.

**The room.** A full-height grid: a 68px header row (58px on phones), then `main` with the stage and a 372px side panel (gap 14px, padding 0 14px 14px). Hiding the panel animates `grid-template-columns` to `minmax(0, 1fr) 0px` over 420ms. The header row sits on flat `bg`; the stage fills the rest and carries the silk.

**The stage's gallery (`justify()` in templates/index.html).** Tiles are absolutely positioned inside the stage and placed by code:
1. **Usable box.** Inset 16px (10px when the stage is under 560px wide), starting 10px below the stage-top stack (green room, solo note, pager) and ending 12px above the dock.
2. **Page size.** 9 tiles when the box is at least 960 by 500, else 6. Pages step with the pager or the arrow keys.
3. **Order.** Raised hands first (earliest hand first), then the most recent speech or reaction, then staff, then camera on, then you, then by name. On page one, tiles already on screen keep their slots; newcomers only fill vacated slots, so nobody jumps around while talking.
4. **Shapes.** Every camera tile is exactly its camera's aspect ratio (`videoWidth / videoHeight`, 16:9 until metadata arrives), so a portrait phone sits beside landscape laptops uncropped. Camera-off cards are elastic between 3:4 and 2:1 (0.75 to 2.0): they give way so rows fit, then stretch to fill them.
5. **Rows.** It tries every way of breaking the page into rows (2^(n-1) splits), takes the tallest row height that fits every row, and scores each layout by total tile area times (0.85 + 0.15 × evenness), evenness being the narrowest row width over the widest. The winner is centred on both axes, with a 12px gap (8px under 560px).
6. **Pinned.** A pinned person takes the whole box alone, and the pager reads "Pinned · double-click to unpin".

**Share mode.** When someone presents, the stage ground turns to `screen`, the silk fades to 12%, and the shared screen fills the stage (inset 14px, bottom clear of the dock, `object-fit: contain`, 18px radius). Everyone moves to a filmstrip on the right: 196px wide, 10px gaps, each camera in its own aspect ratio via `--ar` (camera-off faces at 5:4), up to 240px tall, the strip fading out over its last 14%. On phones the filmstrip becomes a 96px-tall horizontal strip above the dock, fading at its right end.

**Breakpoints as built.** 640px (sheets become bottom sheets, lobby HUD compacts), 680px (dashboard phone), 720px (topbar compacts to the mark and avatar), 760px (archive phone: the timeline's name column narrows to 84px), 860px (room phone: the side panel becomes a bottom sheet, the dock scrolls, the filmstrip turns horizontal), 900px (dashboard hero stacks), 980px (door stacks), 1080px (recording page stacks), 1100px (the room's me pill drops its name). The room also measures the stage itself (560px, and 960 by 500) rather than the window.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is a hybrid of three things: soft offset shadows, glass over moving imagery, and tonal steps from mist to paper to wells. Shadows are tinted violet-ink by day (rgb 45 38 99) and pure black by night, always pushed downward with a negative spread so they pool under an object instead of haloing around it. Coloured emphasis never glows; it rings.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Rest** (`--shadow-1`: `0 1px 2px rgb(45 38 99 / 0.05), 0 8px 20px -12px rgb(45 38 99 / 0.2)`; night `0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.3), 0 8px 20px -12px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)`): buttons, chips on posters, cards at rest, active segments, the join and search pills.
- **Raised** (`--shadow-2`: `0 2px 6px rgb(45 38 99 / 0.05), 0 24px 48px -24px rgb(45 38 99 / 0.3)`; night `0 2px 6px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.3), 0 24px 48px -24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7)`): heroes and bands, room tiles, the side panel, the lobby HUD, hovered cards and posters, the focused Ask bar.
- **Floating** (`--shadow-3`: `0 4px 10px rgb(45 38 99 / 0.06), 0 50px 90px -40px rgb(45 38 99 / 0.42)`; night `0 4px 10px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.35), 0 50px 90px -40px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.85)`): the door card and studio, the spotlight, sheets, menus, the toast, the dock, a speaking tile.
- **Primary lift** (`inset 0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 0.18)` plus Rest): the one inner highlight, on violet buttons.
- **Focus** (`0 0 0 4px var(--violet-soft)` with a `violet-line` border on fields and bars; `outline: 2px solid var(--violet)`, offset 2px, for everything else via `:focus-visible`).
- **Glass recipe.** `backdrop-filter: blur(14px to 28px) saturate(1.3 to 1.5)` on `glass` or `glass-strong` with a 1px `glass-line` edge: 28px on the door card, 22 to 24px on the studio, spotlight and dock, 14 to 20px on pills and bars, 10px on chips over posters.
- **Video glass.** Over live video the glass goes dark: `rgb(14 11 32)` at 0.5 to 0.62 with a 10 to 16px blur and white text (name pills, the pin button, filmstrip labels, the presenting badge, player controls).

### Named Rules
**The Ring, Not Glow Rule.** Coloured emphasis is a crisp zero-blur ring (2 to 4.5px, often with a 2px `surface` gap inside it): the speaking face, a raised hand, the selected type card, chapter dots. The only blurred shadows are the three offset depth shadows above.

**The Glass Over Motion Rule.** Glass is for things that float over silk, video, the stage, a poster, or content scrolling under the sticky topbar. Anything that is read at length sits on paper.

## Shapes

The form language is soft and nested. Every control is a pill or a circle; containers are rounded rectangles whose radius steps down as they nest, so an inner shape never out-rounds its parent.

- **Pill and circle** (`pill` 999px, or 50%): buttons, chips, fields inside bars, segmented tracks and items, the dock and its controls, name pills, toasts, badges, avatars. Avatars and faces are always circles.
- **Hero** (34px): the door card, the dashboard hero, archive bands (28px on phones). The studio panel is 32px, the lobby's game-over card 30px.
- **Panel** (28px): sheets (28px 28px 0 0 as phone bottom sheets), the stage (24px on phones), the side panel, archive panels.
- **Card** (26px): meeting cards and the spotlight. **Poster** (24px): archive posters, empty states, the chat input. **Tile** (22px): room tiles, the green room bar, dashboard recording posters.
- **Media** (20px): the player box, the studio caption, Ask answers. **Inset** (18px): rows and blocks inside panels (studio lanes, menus, type cards, option groups, invite rows, hand rows). **Plate** (16px): voice plates, filmstrip thumbs, participant rows, transcript lines. **Field** (14px, the only `--r-md` reference): text fields, chapter rows, leaderboard rows. **Item** (12px): menu items, reaction buttons. **Tip** (9px): tooltips and timeline tracks.
- **Chat bubbles** are pills with one sharp corner toward the speaker: 6px 18px 18px 18px for others, 18px 6px 18px 18px for yours.
- **Masks do real work.** The 8x.social serif mark is a CSS mask over `currentColor`, so the lockup takes any ink. The speaking ring and the Ask bar's focus border are the silk gradient masked to a 3px or 1.5px frame (`mask-composite: exclude`).
- **Proportions.** Posters are 16:10, the spotlight poster 16:9, camera-off filmstrip faces 5:4.


### Named Rules
**The Pill Rule.** If it can be pressed, it is a pill or a circle. Corners belong to containers.

**The Step-Down Rule.** Radii shrink as surfaces nest (34, 28, 26 to 22, 20 to 16, 14 to 9), so an inner shape is always tighter than the one around it.

## Components

### Motion grammar
- **Easing.** `--ease` `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, an exponential ease-out, is the only easing in use.
- **Durations.** `--d1` 160ms for colour, background and border changes; `--d2` 260ms for shadows, fades, the switch knob and presses; `--d3` 420ms for hover lifts, the side panel folding and the toast's travel.
- **Entrances.** `meets-rise` (from opacity 0, 14px lower, scale 0.98) for sheets (380ms), menus (260ms), cards (600ms), heroes and bands (700ms), the spotlight (800ms, 120ms late), the door card (900ms) and the studio (1100ms, 160ms late). `meets-fade` for overlays; `meets-sheet` (from 100% below, 420ms) for phone bottom sheets.
- **Authored moments.** The theme reveal (a circle of the new light growing from the switch, 640ms); the silk ring arriving on a speaker (240ms fade) while their face's halo tracks their voice level (140ms linear); room tiles gliding to their new rectangles (left, top, width and height over 480ms) and new tiles growing in from 0.92 (560ms); the studio's active lane bouncing (scaleY 0.45 to 1, 820ms alternate, staggered 71ms per bar).
- **Presses.** Buttons drop 1px and scale to 0.985; dock controls and the send button scale to 0.94.
- **Signals.** Live and recording dots pulse a 7px ring every 1.9s; the lobby's waiting dot and a struggling connection glyph breathe in opacity.
- **Reduced motion.** A global rule in `meets.css` cuts every animation and transition to 0.001ms; silk videos are unloaded (the poster image stays), the studio stops rotating, and the theme switches instantly.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** route every action through violet: primary buttons, send, a switch that is on, the selected category, screen share when on, a pinned tile, chapter dots, and in-page links in `violet-text`.
- **Do** give a voice its colour by setting `--tc: var(--tN)` on the element and mixing from it (`color-mix(in oklab, var(--tc) 30%, transparent)`); assign N only through `Meets.track(rank)` or the room's `TRACK_ORDER` (0, 2, 3, 1, 4, 5, 6, 7), by join order in the room and talk time in the archive.
- **Do** keep every camera tile at its camera's own aspect ratio and let only camera-off cards stretch, between 3:4 and 2:1.
- **Do** keep tiles that are on screen in their slots; newcomers (raised hands, recent speakers) fill vacated slots only.
- **Do** declare any new colour in both the `:root` and `[data-theme="dark"]` blocks and have components read the token, so both lights stay first-class.
- **Do** build silk surfaces as a `.silk` panel with a `video[data-silk]` carrying both `data-light` and `data-dark` sources, and call `window.meetsSilk()` after injecting one.
- **Do** set times, codes, durations and percentages in Geist Mono with tabular numerals.
- **Do** write every label, button and heading in sentence case.
- **Do** give every icon-only control an `aria-label` and a `data-tip` tooltip (the room's `tip()` helper sets both).
- **Do** honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: the global rule in `meets.css`, no silk video (poster only), no rotating studio, an instant theme switch.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use em dashes in product copy; the build keeps them to code comments. Use a comma, a colon or a full stop.
- **Don't** put arrows on buttons or links ("Open the archive", not "Open the archive" followed by an arrow). Icon-only controls such as sign out and the pager chevrons draw their own icon.
- **Don't** leave a background plain: pages sit on the mist, the silk or the lobby sky, and the room's stage carries the silk poster.
- **Don't** put CSS filters on playing video. Bake a tint into the footage (the night silk is its own graded file) and swap sources; the only filters in the build are drop shadows on reaction emoji.
- **Don't** crop portrait cameras: no fixed 16:9 slots, no cover-fitting a camera into a differently shaped tile; the shared screen uses `contain`.
- **Don't** give a voice red, amber or green; red and amber already carry state, and green is held in reserve.
- **Don't** show anyone else's muted mic in red; only your own mic in the dock turns red.
- **Don't** add blurred glows or coloured halos; emphasis is a crisp ring, and depth is the three offset shadows.
- **Don't** set labels in uppercase or with positive letter-spacing.
- **Don't** present the poster waveforms as audio: their heights are seeded shapes, and only lane order, lane length (talk share) and colour carry data.
- **Don't** use the demo portraits in `static/people/` anywhere but the sign-in illustration.
